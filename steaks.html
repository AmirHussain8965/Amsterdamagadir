THE GASTRONOMIC RITUAL

Tableside Carving & Wood Smoke Ceremony

Dining at Amsterdam Luxury is not merely a meal; it is a dramatic live performance. Every signature steak and imperial tomahawk cut is unveiled tableside under an aromatic glass cloche, gently kissed with aromatic wood smoke and carved with artisanal precision.

Finished with 24-karat gold leaf flakes, smoked sea salt crystals, and drizzled with warm bone marrow truffle butter before your eyes.
