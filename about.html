THE STORY BEHIND AMSTERDAM

A Sanctuary of Flavor & Spectacle

Amsterdam Luxury was founded with a singular ambition: to provide Agadir with an internationally recognized dining and entertainment destination.

From the golden glow of our crystal chandeliers to the sizzle of 45-day dry-aged halal steaks seared over fragrant olive charcoal, every element is curated to transcend standard restaurant dining. We blend the precision of European steakhouse craftsmanship with the vibrancy of live stage artistry and timeless Moroccan hospitality.

THE CRAFT OF AGING

45-Day Dry Aging & Charcoal Mastery

Our butchery program is governed by rigorous culinary standards. Our prime steaks rest inside specialized Himalayan salt-brick aging chambers for 45 to 60 days, concentrating rich umami notes and yielding unparalleled tenderness.
