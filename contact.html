House Etiquette & Services

To maintain our distinguished atmosphere, we kindly request our guests to note the following guidelines.

Dress Code

Smart Elegant. Collared shirts, tailored attire, and evening dresses are encouraged. Sportswear, beachwear, caps, and athletic shoes are not permitted.

Valet Concierge

Complimentary dedicated valet parking is available at our main entrance on Boulevard du 20 Août for all dining and dinner show guests.

Table Hold Policy

Reserved tables are held for a maximum of 20 minutes past the confirmed booking time. Please notify us via WhatsApp if you anticipate any delays.
